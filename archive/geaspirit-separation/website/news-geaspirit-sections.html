The GeaSpirit Score — explainable, not a black box

A transparent 0–100 triage signal built from four independent 0–25 dimensions, each with an honest HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW confidence band:

  • SIGNAL — open satellite imagery (Sentinel-2 / Landsat) blended with regional geological favourability. An apparent indication, never a confirmed deposit — only drilling proves ore.
  • ACCESS / DEPTH — surface reachability and depth, weighted by infrastructure proximity; remote or very deep targets score lower.
  • PRECISION — how tightly the target is bounded in space (quality of coordinates and classification).
  • CERTAINTY — validation strength minus a missing-data penalty; drives the confidence band. Pilot zones get conservative spatial cross-validation; every other asset is an evidence-fusion estimate.

Why second-chance assets

  • Economic, not geologic — closed on low prices, bankruptcy, dated technology or shallow historical drilling; the ore may still be there.
  • Tailings & waste — historic tailings and waste from old mines can hold recoverable critical minerals under modern processing (a reprocessing opportunity, not a guarantee).
  • Modern eyes — satellite data, open geoscience and explainable scoring re-read assets with tools the original miners never had; precise coordinates are stored so the GeaSpirit engine can re-examine them over time.
