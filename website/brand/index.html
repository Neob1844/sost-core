Guidelines

Using the marks

The short version. The full guide is in the kit and as PDF or Markdown.

Do

  • Scale uniformly. The logo is a circle and stays a circle.
  • Keep clear space of at least 10 % of the ring diameter on every side.
  • Prefer black or very dark backgrounds; use the transparent files on light or coloured ones.
  • Use at least 24 px on screen (32 px+ recommended; Σ stays legible at 32 px) and 10 mm in print.
  • Write the names exactly: SOST and wSOST.

Don’t

  • Stretch, skew, rotate, recolour, crop the ring or add effects.
  • Redraw, move or swap the Σ / wΣ symbols, or use one logo for the other asset.
  • Recreate the logos with AI or vector tracing and present them as official.
  • Use the legacy red-eye emblem as the current logo.
  • Present wSOST as live, listed or tradable, or publish a wSOST contract address before an official announcement.
  • Combine the marks with retired elements (gold-backing guarantees, Gold Vault, XAUT, Gold Access Key) or imply endorsement or returns.

Legacy

Superseded emblems

Until 12 November 2026 SOST used a red eye with a luminous Σ, and wSOST a red eye with WΣ. They are kept for reference only. Do not use them as the current logo.
