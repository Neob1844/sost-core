# SOST Brand Guidelines

Version 2.0 · 12 November 2026 · https://sostcore.com/brand/

**What changed in 2.0:** the official primary logo of SOST is now the **rainbow Sigma** (Σ inside a multicolour energy ring). wSOST uses the same ring with **wΣ**. The former red-eye emblems are **Legacy / Superseded**: they remain in the kit for reference only and must not be presented as the current logo.

## 2. The logos

The two logos are the official artwork supplied by the SOST project. Nothing has been redrawn or regenerated: every file is the original image, placed on a square canvas centred on the ring and resampled in high quality. Outside the ring the files are fully transparent.

- `sost/original/`, `wsost/original/`: the original artwork, byte-identical to the file supplied by the project (1254 × 1254 px).
- `sost/png/`, `wsost/png/`: 2048 / 1024 / 512 / 256 / 128 / 64 / 32 px, each **transparent** and on **black**. The 2048 px file is enlarged from the 1254 px original.
- `exchange/`: square, transparent token icons, 1024 / 512 / 256 / 200 / 128 / 64 / 32 px, for exchange listings, wallets and token lists.
- `sost/favicon/`: favicon.ico (16 / 32 / 48), favicon PNGs, Apple touch icon, Android / PWA icons including maskable versions (logo inside the 80 % safe zone).
- `sost/social/`: Open Graph / social card, 1200 × 630.
- `legacy/eye/`: the former red-eye emblems. **Superseded. Do not use as the current logo.**

**Vector (SVG).** No SVG is provided. The logos are luminous, photographic illustrations (light ribbons, glow, reflections); tracing them into vector paths would visibly change the artwork. The PNG files are the canonical versions.

## 4. Usage rules

- **Proportions.** Always scale uniformly. The logo is a circle and must stay a circle.
- **Clear space.** Keep free space around the ring equal to at least 10 % of its diameter.
- **Backgrounds.** Black or very dark backgrounds are preferred, as on sostcore.com. On light or coloured backgrounds use the transparent files.
- **Minimum size.** 24 px on screen (32 px or more recommended; the Σ stays legible at 32 px). In print, 10 mm minimum.
- **Name next to the logo.** Write SOST and wSOST exactly like this (lower-case w for wSOST).

## 5. Not allowed

- Stretching, skewing, rotating, cropping the ring or changing its proportions.
- Recolouring, adding filters, outlines, shadows or effects.
- Replacing, moving or redrawing the Σ or wΣ symbol, or using the SOST logo for wSOST and vice versa.
- Recreating the logo with AI tools or vector tracing and presenting it as official.
- Using the legacy red-eye emblem as the current SOST or wSOST logo.
- Presenting wSOST as live, listed or tradable, or showing a wSOST contract address, before an official announcement on sostcore.com.
- Combining the marks with retired elements: gold-backing or gold-reserve guarantees, Gold Vault, XAUT, Gold Access Key.
- Suggesting endorsement, partnership or investment returns that SOST has not announced.
