How to install on your device

We picked your device automatically. Choose another one if you are installing somewhere else.

Microsoft Edge

  1. Press Install SOST above, or open the menu ⋯ → Apps → Install SOST.
  2. Confirm with Install.
  3. SOST opens the Explorer in its own window. Pin it to the taskbar or Start menu if you like.

Google Chrome

  1. Press Install SOST above, or the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. No icon? Menu ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app.
  3. Confirm with Install. SOST appears with your other apps.

Safari on macOS (Sonoma or later): File → Add to Dock.

Chrome

  1. Press Install SOST above, or the menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  2. Choose Install and confirm.
  3. The SOST icon appears on your home screen and in the app drawer.

Samsung Internet

  1. Open the menu ≡ → Add page to.
  2. Choose Home screen.
  3. Tap Add.

Safari on iPhone or iPad

  1. Tap Share at the bottom (iPhone) or top (iPad) of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep the name SOST and tap Add.

Apple does not let websites show an install button, so these three taps are the only way. In Chrome or Edge on iOS 16.4 or later, the same Share → Add to Home Screen works.

Firefox and other browsers

Firefox on Android: menu ⋮ → Install (or Add to Home screen). Firefox on a computer cannot install web apps: open sostcore.com/install in Edge or Chrome. You can also simply bookmark sostcore.com: the site and the app are the same.

What you get

The app is sostcore.com in its own window. Nothing more is installed on your device.

Its own window

The SOST icon on your desktop, taskbar, Start menu or home screen. No tabs, no address bar.

Always up to date

Every update to the site reaches the app at once. There is nothing to download or update by hand.

No installer file

No .exe, no .apk, no extension. Your browser installs it, and you can remove it at any time.

Your wallet stays put

The app never reads, moves or copies wallet data. Your keys stay in the browser where you created them.

Remove the app

Windows, macOS, Linux: open SOST → menu ⋯ → Uninstall SOST. If the browser also offers to clear site data, leave it unticked to keep your settings. Android: press and hold the icon → Uninstall. iPhone and iPad: press and hold the icon → Remove App. Removing the app never deletes a wallet.
