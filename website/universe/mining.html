Mine with your CPU. Memory is the work.

ConvergenceX is memory-hard proof of work: the miner builds and holds a large dataset and scratchpad, and walks through it in sequential rounds. Any ordinary computer with enough RAM can mine solo; any node verifies the result quickly.
