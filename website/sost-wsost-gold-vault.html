SOST Atomic Bridge — Atomic Reserve Vault

TARGET ARCHITECTURE · RESEARCH / DEVELOPMENT · NOT ACTIVE · NOT MAINNET DEPLOYED · NOT AUDITED · NOT YET PROVENno contract deployed · no real funds · no launch date · keyless native reserve designed in research (C705): needs two new SOST consensus rules, not in V17

SOST does not intend to mint a new wSOST for every bridge operation. The target architecture uses a preissued, immobilized wSOST reserve and bidirectional atomic swaps. Native SOST received by the bridge forms the corresponding reserve. The principal research problem is making that native reserve non-discretionary and safely redeemable.

C705 FEASIBILITY RESULT · 26 OCT 2026

The reserve can have no key at all — no daemon, no multisig, no guardians. That needs exactly two new SOST consensus rules (keyless reserve outputs + SOST verifying Ethereum finality) and one Ethereum-side component (a SOST header relay). With them, the reserve and the vault each check proofs from the other chain, so the hashlock is no longer what protects the reserve: hashed swaps stay only as an optional fast path for market makers using their own funds. Research only; not implemented; not in V17. See Diagram 7b.

Step by step · and why the entry still needs a verifier
  1. Your wallet creates a secret s locally and opens a request bound to H = sha256(s), the amount and your Ethereum address. The Gold Access Key in XAUT is escrowed in the same Ethereum transaction — if you lack XAUT, it fails here, before any SOST moves.
  2. You lock 1,000 SOST in a native HTLC: hashlock H, claimable by the reserve, refundable to you after ≈288 blocks.
  3. The engine waits for 30 SOST confirmations and checks the lock.
  4. The Atomic Vault moves 1,000 existing wSOST from inventory into an Ethereum HTLC with the same H, payable only to you.
  5. You claim with s: the wSOST reach you and the escrowed XAUT goes to Gold Reserve V2, in one transaction.
  6. The engine uses the now-public s to claim your 1,000 SOST into the native reserve.

Step 4 must not happen for a SOST lock that does not exist — otherwise anyone could collect wSOST with their own secret. So Ethereum still needs to know the SOST lock is real: today through the engine's authorization (which can be a threshold of keys), later possibly through a SOST light client. The atomic swap turns “mint authority” into “release-from-inventory authority”: the damage of a compromised verifier is bounded by the inventory and the caps, not removed.

Design note · a secret revealed in a failed transaction

If the XAUT payment were collected inside the claim and the claim reverted, the secret s would still be public on-chain and the reserve could claim the SOST although you received nothing. That is why the Gold Access Key is escrowed first and the claim itself has no other condition: anyone holding s can submit it, and the wSOST can only go to you.

WHY 0.10% · MIN 0.50 EUR · MAX 250 EUR
Other bridges price something different — relayer capital, liquidity, gas, risk — and their fees vary by route and size, so no other bridge's fee is directly comparable to this contribution. The design choice is: 0.10% so that the reserve grows in proportion to use; a 0.50 EUR minimum that only matters below 500 EUR and stays below the Ethereum gas of the same operation; and a 250 EUR cap, reached at 250,000 EUR, so very large operations are not punished (1,000,000 EUR pays an effective 0.025%) while still contributing meaningfully — the earlier 50 EUR cap would have meant 0.005%. REFERENCE · CONVENTIONAL LOCK / MINT MODEL
This section describes the conventional lock → mint / burn → release model and the C701/C703 prototype built for it. It is not the SOST target architecture — that is the SOST Atomic Bridge. It is kept because its components (verification, single use, recovery states, Gold Access Key, Gold Reserve V2, tests) are reused.
HOW IT WORKS
You lock native SOST in the Bridge Vault; an automated engine verifies the deposit on the SOST chain; you sign one Ethereum transaction that pays the Gold Access Key in XAUT to Gold Reserve V2 and mints the same amount of wSOST to you. Going back, you burn wSOST and the vault releases the same SOST.
WHY SOST IS LOCKED
So that every wSOST has exactly one native SOST behind it. Native SOST is never burned and no new SOST is ever created by the bridge: the coins wait in the vault until someone burns the matching wSOST.
WHY wSOST IS MINTED
Ethereum wallets, exchanges and applications cannot hold native SOST. wSOST is the Ethereum-side representation: an ERC-20 minted only against a verified lock, 1 for 1, and burned on return.
WHAT XAUT DOES
XAUT (tokenized gold) pays the Gold Access Key: 0.10% of the operation value, min 0.50 EUR, max 250 EUR. It moves from your wallet straight to Gold Reserve V2 in the same transaction as the mint. It is a cost of access, not backing of wSOST.
WHAT GOLD RESERVE V2 IS
An Ethereum contract that receives XAUT from completed conversions, publishes every deposit and its balance, allows no arbitrary withdrawal, and can only be paused, rescued to a pre-named quarantine vault, or migrated under guardian + holder rules.
WHAT GOLD RESERVE V2 IS NOT
Not a liquidity pool. It receives no SOST, issues no SOST or wSOST, keeps no peg, sets no price floor, guarantees no price and gives no holder an individual right to the gold. It is separate from the historical Gold Vault.
WHO PAYS GAS
You do: Ethereum gas in ETH for the mint (and for the burn on return), plus the normal SOST network fee of your deposit. SOST does not advance ETH or buy wSOST for anyone. All costs are shown before you sign.
WHY NO LIQUIDITY POOL IS NEEDED
Nobody has to pay you from pre-funded capital: the wSOST you receive is minted against your own locked SOST. So there is no LP capital, no rebalancing fee, no slippage, and the amount is always exactly 1:1.
HOW AUTOMATIC VERIFICATION WORKS
The engine runs its own SOST node. It finds the deposit by its capsule C701:0x<request id> and checks the vault address, the exact amount, the request, the Ethereum recipient, 30 confirmations, that the output is unspent and never used before. Only then it signs one EIP-712 authorization.
WHY A HASH ALONE IS NOT PROOF
A transaction hash says nothing about who should receive wSOST, and anyone can copy it. The bridge never mints because of a hash: it uses it only as a lookup key in recovery. The proof is the engine's own reading of the chain plus an authorization bound to one recipient, amount and deposit.
WHAT HAPPENS IF SOMETHING FAILS
On Ethereum, the XAUT payment and the mint are one transaction: both or neither. Across the two chains it is not atomic, so a locked deposit without a mint stays pending, can be re-authorized, or refunded after the request is revoked on Ethereum. See Example 4.
HOW wSOST RETURNS TO SOST
Any wSOST holder — including one who bought on an exchange — burns wSOST with a SOST address; after the burn is final the vault releases the same amount minus the SOST network fee. No XAUT on the return.
SECURITY MODEL
Authorizations bound to chain id, contract, recipient, amount, deposit, expiry and nonce; each deposit usable once; per-mint, daily and total caps; signed, fresh, bounded prices; reconciliation that pauses minting; separate keys for minting, prices and administration; 2-day timelock on configuration; an event log the engine only appends to (an enforced append-only audit trail is still to be built).
CENTRALIZATION LIMITATION
Ethereum cannot read the SOST chain, so in the first version a single automated verification component decides that a deposit is real. That is a central point of trust: the bridge is not trustless. A stolen verifier key must not be able to mint unbacked wSOST; until that is demonstrable, the bridge stays off.
FUTURE: V17 MULTISIG
Native SOST multisig is planned for V17 (block #40,000). The target is a 3-of-5 Bridge Vault, with vault spending keys separate from the Ethereum authorization key, so that compromising one does not compromise the other. Until then the vault could only be a single cold key.
AUDIT STATUS
Not audited. Contracts and engine exist as development prototypes with tests (Foundry 87/87, engine 25/25), on a development branch only. An independent security audit and a legal / regulatory review are open blockers.

COMPARISON

How the SOST Atomic Bridge differs from most bridges

Most cross-chain bridges belong to one of five families. They differ in who decides that a deposit is real, where the money for the other side comes from, and who keeps the fee.

1 · CUSTODIAN / MULTISIG LOCK-AND-MINT
A company or a small group of keys holds the original coins and mints the wrapped token. Example: WBTC (custodian-held BTC). What went wrong elsewhere: Ronin (March 2022) — attackers obtained 5 of its 9 validator keys and took about 625 million USD; Harmony Horizon (June 2022) — 2 of 5 multisig keys, about 100 million USD.
2 · LIQUIDITY NETWORKS
No wrapping: pools or relayers on the destination chain pay you from their own capital, and are repaid later. Examples: Stargate (a per-transfer fee shared by treasury, token holders and LPs, plus pool rebalancing), Across (an LP fee set by the protocol plus a relayer fee for gas, capital and risk), Hop, Synapse. Fees pay the capital, so they vary by route and size.
3 · GUARDIAN / MESSAGING NETWORKS
An external set of validators signs messages that let a contract mint on the other side. Examples: Wormhole (a guardian set, 13 of 19 signatures), LayerZero (verifier networks). What went wrong: Wormhole (February 2022) — a signature-verification bug let an attacker mint 120,000 wrapped ETH without a deposit.
4 · ISSUER BURN-AND-MINT
The issuer of the asset burns it on one chain and mints it on the other. Example: Circle CCTP for USDC — almost free (destination gas only) because the issuer itself attests. Only possible for the issuer's own token.
5 · CANONICAL ROLLUP / LIGHT-CLIENT
The destination chain verifies the source chain's state itself. Example: the Optimism Standard Bridge — deposits in minutes, withdrawals after a 7-day challenge window. The most trust-minimized model, but it exists only where one chain can verify the other.
SOST ATOMIC BRIDGE (TARGET)
Atomic swaps between native SOST and a preissued, immobilized wSOST inventory: no mint per operation, no burn on the return, no liquidity pool, no outside federation or validator network, and a Gold Access Key in XAUT that goes 100% to Gold Reserve V2. In its keyless form (C705) it joins family 5: the vault releases only against a proof of a SOST deposit, and SOST pays only against a proof of an Ethereum return — no key, no multisig. Honest limits: that needs two new SOST consensus rules and a SOST header relay on Ethereum, none built — which is why it is not approved for production.

ACCESS LAYER

Why wSOST — and why native SOST stays the real asset

RESEARCH NOT DEPLOYED

The integration barrier

One practical barrier to wider SOST market access is the technical burden a centralized exchange faces when it integrates a new, independent Layer-1 blockchain. Listing native SOST would require the exchange to:

  • run and maintain a SOST full node;
  • integrate SOST deposits and withdrawals;
  • implement a SOST-specific confirmation and reorganisation policy;
  • monitor wallet and node infrastructure around the clock;
  • review SOST RPC and transaction semantics;
  • build custody and operational tooling for a new chain;
  • carry the security and maintenance work specific to SOST.

Ethereum and ERC-20 infrastructure, on the other hand, is already in place at virtually every major centralized exchange: deposits, withdrawals, custody and monitoring for ERC-20 tokens are standard operations there. For such a venue, integrating an ERC-20 representation is operationally much simpler than integrating a new chain.

WHAT wSOST IS FOR
wSOST is intended to reduce integration friction for venues that already support Ethereum/ERC-20 infrastructure. It is being researched as an access layer. It does not guarantee that any exchange will list it, and listing, liquidity, legal review and exchange due diligence remain independent requirements.

RESEARCH NOT ACTIVE NOT DEPLOYED NEEDS A HARD FORK AFTER V17

A proposed use for the XAUT collected by the Gold Access Key: periodically buy wSOST back from the market and take the same amount of native SOST out of circulation forever. THE CONSTITUTIONAL RULE
For every N wSOST permanently retired on Ethereum, exactly N native SOST are permanently immobilized on SOST. In base units, with no rounding and no fee taken out of either side.

How it would work — option A (chosen): permanent immobilization, no burn

1 · GOLD ACCESS KEY XAUT from bridge entries accumulates in Gold Reserve V2 2 · BUYBACK periodically, anyone can trigger a capped purchase of wSOST with that XAUT (max price, max slippage, max size; no safe liquidity → no purchase, the XAUT stays) 3 · RETIRED INVENTORY the wSOST bought go to a permanent retired inventory on Ethereum that has no exit: they are never sold, lent, re-issued or returned to the market 4 · DEAD VAULT after that step is final, exactly the same amount of native SOST moves from the bridge reserve into the SOST Dead Vault — an output type nobody can ever spend

Option A keeps the founding rule of the SOST Atomic Bridge: wSOST is issued once and its total supply never changes. The retired wSOST still exist on Ethereum, but they sit in a contract with no way out, and they are counted separately from the ordinary inventory. // BEACON

Beacon — Advisory Channel

Signed operator notices, propagated between nodes. Advisory only. Does not affect mining rewards, block validity, chain selection, transaction validation, wallet balances, PoPC, Gold Vault, or consensus.

Phase II-A — local notices

Each node reads notices.json on startup. ECDSA-SHA256 signature verification against a hardcoded operator pubkey. Failure modes (bad signature, wrong network, expired, malformed) are silent drops. Active at V13_HEIGHT (12,000).

Phase II-B — threshold + revocation

Adds N-of-M threshold signatures (default 3-of-5), revocation by notice ID (only threshold-signed notices can revoke), and an optional mirror_url metadata field (never fetched by the node). Active at V13_HEIGHT.

Phase III — P2P gossip

Nodes gossip verified notices via a BCNN dispatcher with hard limits: 4 KB max notice, 32-notice LRU dedup cache, 8 notices/peer/min rate limit. Bad-signature notices are silently dropped; oversized/malformed/rate-limit hits earn peer misbehavior. Active at V13_HEIGHT.

Hard invariant. No Beacon code path links into block validation, mining validity, or chain commit. Beacon cannot reject, invalidate, or affect the canonical chain. Rollback is a single-line constant revert (BEACON_P2P_ACTIVATION_HEIGHT to INT64_MAX in include/sost/params.h).
