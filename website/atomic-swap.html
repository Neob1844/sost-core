    How it works — and what it is not

    An atomic swap locks your SOST in a self-custodial lockbox (an HTLC) that has exactly two exits: the counterparty can take it only by revealing a secret that also releases your funds on the other chain, or — if nobody completes in time — the lockbox refunds you. No custodian ever holds your money, and the two legs either both happen or neither does.

    This page never asks for a seed phrase or private key, never signs, and never uploads your secret. Live execution runs through your own wallet / node in the developer console.

    Status meanings: Available = usable by anyone. Founder testing = wired up but the contract is undeployed & unaudited, so it's founder-only. Coming soon = not yet built (e.g. BTC lands with V15). Disabled = intentionally off (unsafe token type). Right now nothing is Available — this is a preview.
