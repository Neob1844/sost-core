The ideaWhat Autonomous Useful Compute is

Proof-of-Work secures SOST. Autonomous Useful Compute is SOST’s principal direction for economic utility: a market in which computation that someone actually needs is requested, executed by independent machines, checked, and paid for in SOST — with rules that run automatically instead of an operator deciding who is paid.

It is not a new coin, not a mining pool and not a replacement for Proof-of-Work. Mining keeps securing the chain; the compute market would add a reason to hold and use SOST.

Step by stepThe market cycle

  1. Quote. The customer describes the job (software, inputs, size) and the verification method, and receives a price estimate.
  2. Deposit. The customer funds Compute Credits: a prepaid deposit of SOST reserved for the job.
  3. Match. The market selects providers whose hardware fits the job and whose asking price is within the customer’s limit.
  4. Assign. The job is split into work units, each sent to one or more providers.
  5. Execute. Providers run the work units on their CPUs or GPUs with the reference software.
  6. Verify. Results are checked objectively — by recomputation, proofs or redundant execution — wherever a reliable method exists.
  7. Settle. Verified results go to the customer and SOST is released from the deposit to the providers under the contract rules.
  8. Close. Work that fails verification is not paid; the unused part of the deposit stays available to the customer.

PaymentsSOST Compute Credits

Compute Credits are prepaid compute deposits. A customer sets aside SOST for future jobs and the balance is drawn down as verified work settles. Credits are a deposit — no new token is created, and no SOST is minted for them.

V17The Useful Compute Vault (20 %)

V17 proposes that 20 % of each block subsidy accumulates in the Useful Compute Vault, a consensus balance with no address and no key. It does not increase the maximum supply of 4,669,201.54789641 SOST. In V17 the vault cannot be spent; a later, separately tested activation would allow it to pay only for verifiable protocol services, with a cap on how much can be used. Customer payments for commercial compute are separate: they come from the customers’ own deposits, not from the vault.

QuestionsShort answers

  • Can I earn SOST with my computer today? Only by mining. The compute market is not open.
  • Does V17 open the market? No. V17 would start the Useful Compute Vault; using it, and opening the market, need later authorised activations.
  • Is there a compute token? No. Compute Credits are deposits of SOST.
  • Who decides who gets paid? The goal is that rules do: a provider is paid when its result passes verification.
