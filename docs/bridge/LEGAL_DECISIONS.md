# Legal decisions required before any public step (not legal advice)

Engineering may proceed on devnet and, if authorised, on testnet. The questions below must be
answered by qualified counsel **before** any mainnet pilot, public custody, public sale or public
claim. Until then every public text keeps the status RESEARCH / NOT DEPLOYED.

| # | Question | Why it matters | Current design position |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Does holding users' SOST in the Bridge Vault constitute crypto-asset custody requiring authorisation (e.g. a MiCA CASP)? | custody is a regulated service in the EU | pilot only after an entity and jurisdiction are chosen; capped exposure |
| L2 | Who is the issuer of wSOST, and does issuing it to the public require a white paper / legal person? | offer of a crypto-asset to the public | issuer entity to be decided; no public issuance in Stage 1 |
| L3 | Could any communication make wSOST or SOST an asset-referenced token? | ARTs carry authorisation, reserve and redemption duties | wSOST references only native SOST 1:1; no gold peg, floor or redemption is ever claimed |
| L4 | Is selling existing treasury wSOST for XAUT (the Gold → wSOST desk) an offer to the public or an exchange service? | it is a sale, not a donation | desk designed and GATED OFF |
| L5 | Do permanent contributions to Gold Reserve V2 create tax or ownership issues for any natural person? | gift-tax / ownership of donated assets | contributions go to a contract with no owner and no withdrawal |
| L6 | PAXG availability in the EU; XAUT terms of use for this purpose | issuer terms and regional restrictions | PAXG feature-gated off; XAUT terms to be reviewed |
| L7 | Is the governance vote a form of collective investment decision? | AIF definitions | the vote only authorises migrating the reserve between identical vaults; no return, no distribution |
| L8 | Advertising rules for crypto-assets (e.g. CNMV Circular 1/2022 in Spain) | public communication | neutral, factual material with risk warnings |
| L9 | US persons and securities law | yield-like features | no yield features; geographic restrictions to be considered |
| L10 | Sanctions / AML screening of large contributions and bridge users | compliance; issuers can freeze tainted funds | policy to be written before any pilot |
