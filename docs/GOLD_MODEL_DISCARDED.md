# Gold model: discarded (16 November 2026)

**SOST has no gold backing, no committed reserves and no obligation to acquire, hold or contribute gold, XAUT or PAXG.** There is no convertibility, redemption right or guarantee linked to gold or to any external asset. Earlier foundation commitments to contribute XAUT or PAXG (including 0.6 XAUT + 0.6 PAXG and the FOUND-00x PoPC commitments) are **withdrawn**.

## Current model
- Proof of Work (ConvergenceX) + **DTD** and **DTD Jackpot**, which distribute SOST itself. They are not backing mechanisms.
- V16 (active): 50% miner / 50% DTD since block #25,000.
- V17 (proposed, **not active**): 80% miner / 15% DTD / 5% Jackpot.
- The Gold Funding Vault and PoPC Pool are consensus addresses that received 25% each of the reward for blocks 0–24,999. Since #25,000 they receive nothing. Their balances are **SOST, not gold**, and are spent only by the DTD Jackpot rule.

## Gold price on the website
Any gold price or the gold reference (1 SOST ≡ 0.8886707657 mg of gold) shown on sostcore.com is informational only: not a market price, not backing, not a guarantee, not a redemption or exchange right and not an economic commitment. It is not part of SOST tokenomics.

## Historical documents
Documents in this repository that describe a gold reserve, a Heritage Reserve, XAUT/PAXG purchases, gold-custody PoPC models, metal tokens or foundation gold commitments describe **abandoned plans**. They are kept only as a historical record and are not in force.
